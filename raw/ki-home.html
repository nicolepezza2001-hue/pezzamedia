FAQ

Do I need to be an experienced cook?

Look, it obviously won’t hurt, it would be silly to pretend otherwise, but we know you’ve got it in you, and what’s the worst case scenario, you burn your Soufflé?

Is this a game or a challenge?

It’s definitely a challenge, but we’re not going to stop you from making it a game if you like to live dangerously. Some chefs like to take a drink every time they make a mistake, and some whenever they get something right, either way you’ll have a good time.

How long do the recipes actually take

Anywhere from a quick-ish evening to a full weekend project. Every card lists its time estimate up front so you know what you’re getting into.

Who is this for?

Kitchen Impossible was made for people that desperately need to learn how to cook through a trial by fire, for the foodies that should spend more time cooking than eating, and the cooks that think they can do better than the chef on TV.

Does the Chef’s hat come in different sizes?

Our chef’s hat has an adjustable velcro strap that makes fitting it to your giant (or tiny) brain very easy. We haven’t found a head too big or too small for it yet so you or the recipient will be fine!

Are the recipes authentic to where the dishes come from?

Not only are these dishes authentic, many of them require real traditional techniques and ingredients, no shortcuts that water down the difficulty. If you don’t recognise the flag for the country of origin, we encourage you to improve your flag knowledge.
