A bit about me

Hey, I’m Dan 👋

I’ve written for Jägermeister, helped a Web3 launch pull in $16M in its first week, and scaled a Shopify brand 33x. But my favourite part of the job is seeing brands flourish and founders breathe a sigh of relief when they’re back on track.

After 10+ years across agency, in-house and Fractional CMO roles, I’ve learned the gap between an average store and an incredible one is rarely the product. It’s the dozens of small details (the headline, the offer, the product page, the checkout, the follow-up email) that decide whether a visitor buys or bounces. Getting every one of them right is what I do.

I studied Economics and Behavioural Psychology in Australia, but now I split my time across Europe, the US and Asia. My work is measured by results, numbers and growth, not flashy sales calls and empty promises.

My friends even turned my name into a verb. To Daniel someone is to cut through the fluff and get honest about what’s really holding them back. Fair warning: that’s exactly how I’ll treat your website. I’m not going to sugarcoat anything. The faster it’s fixed, the faster you succeed.

FAQ

What happens on the free site walkthrough?

We jump on a video call and I go through your homepage and a key product page with you, live. I’ll point out the biggest conversion leaks as we go and how I’d fix them, so you leave with a shortlist you can act on yourself. No obligation to work together.

How quickly will I see results?

Quick wins like copy, page fixes and email flows usually go live in the first few weeks. Bigger gains from testing, ads and strategy build over the following months.

What’s the difference between CRO and a Fractional CMO?

CRO focuses on getting more of your existing visitors to buy. As a Fractional CMO I look after the whole marketing engine (ads, email, copy, launches and strategy) part-time, for a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire.

Do you only work with Shopify stores?

Most of my e-commerce clients are on Shopify, but I work with all kinds of businesses, from fintech and telecommunications to SaaS, services and Web3 launches. The principles of why people buy don’t change.

How involved do I need to be?

After a kickoff call and access to your store, analytics and ad accounts, I take the lead. You’ll get regular updates and a quick check-in so you always know what’s happening and why.

What if it doesn’t work?

Every recommendation is tied to a number we agree on up front, so you’ll always know whether it’s working. And the free walkthrough costs you nothing, so you can see how I think before spending a cent.
